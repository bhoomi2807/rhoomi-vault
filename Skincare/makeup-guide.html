Full-face routine

Step-by-Step Makeup

A complete order for a smooth, long-wearing makeup look. Use thin layers and let skincare settle before moving into complexion products.

Prep

  1. Toner

    Pat a hydrating toner onto clean skin. Let it absorb instead of layering the next product immediately.

  2. Moisturizer

    Apply a light, even layer suited to your skin type. Give it 2-3 minutes to settle.

  3. Sunscreen

    Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher as the final skincare layer. Allow it to set for 5-10 minutes.

  4. Primer

    Press a small amount onto areas where makeup fades, pores are visible, or extra hydration is needed.

Base

  1. Foundation

    Start at the center of the face and blend outward with a brush, sponge, or fingers. Build only where needed.

  2. Concealer

    Dot under the eyes and over blemishes or discoloration. Let it sit briefly, then tap the edges to blend.

Shape + Color

  1. Contour

    Add subtle shadow beneath cheekbones, along the jaw, and at the temples. Blend upward, not downward.

  2. Bronzer

    Warm the high points and perimeter of the face: forehead, upper cheeks, and lightly across the nose.

  3. Blush

    Apply to the apples or upper cheekbones and blend toward the temples for a lifted finish.

  4. Highlighter

    Tap onto cheekbones, the inner eye corners, and cupid's bow. Keep texture-heavy areas subtle.

  5. Setting Powder

    Press a light layer under the eyes and through the T-zone. Avoid heavy powder where the skin is dry.

Eyes

  1. Eyeshadow

    Blend a transition shade through the crease, deepen the outer corner, then add a lid shade.

  2. Eyeliner

    Draw close to the lash line. Build the wing in short strokes rather than one long line.

  3. Mascara

    Curl lashes if desired, then wiggle mascara from roots to tips. Let the first coat set before adding another.

Lips + Finish

  1. Lip Primer

    Apply a thin layer to smooth the lips and improve wear. Blot away excess before lining.

  2. Lip Liner

    Trace the natural lip line, then softly shade inward so lipstick fades evenly.

  3. Lipstick or Gloss

    Apply lipstick from the center outward. Add gloss alone or only at the center for dimension.

  4. Setting Spray

    Hold 8-10 inches away and mist in an X and T pattern. Let it air-dry without touching the face.

Cream versus powder order

The sequence above assumes cream or liquid contour, bronzer, blush, and highlighter, so they go before setting powder. If yours are powders, apply setting powder immediately after concealer, then use the powder contour, bronzer, blush, and highlighter on top.
