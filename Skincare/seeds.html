Food preparation

Daily Food Prep

Simple preparation makes seeds and vegetables easier to use and adds garlic to the routine. Start with small portions and drink enough water as you increase fiber.

Soaked Chia Seeds

Soak before eating

Mix 1 tbsp chia seeds with 1/2 cup liquid. Stir, wait 5 minutes, stir again, then soak for at least 20 minutes or overnight in the refrigerator.

Add the gel to oatmeal, yogurt, or smoothies. Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 5 days.

Ground Flax Seeds

Lightly toast + grind

Toast whole flax seeds in a dry pan over low heat for 3-5 minutes, stirring constantly. Cool completely, then grind in a spice grinder.

Use 1-2 tbsp in oatmeal, yogurt, dough, or smoothies. Store ground flax airtight in the refrigerator to protect its oils.

Cooked Vegetables

Steam + season

Steam vegetables until tender but still colorful, usually 4-10 minutes depending on their size and firmness. Dense vegetables may need longer.

Finish with olive oil, lemon juice, herbs, and a pinch of salt. Refrigerate cooked vegetables within 2 hours and use within 3-4 days.

Crushed Garlic Water

Crush + steep

Crush 1 small garlic clove and let it rest for 10 minutes. Add it to 1 cup of warm, not boiling, water and steep for 5 minutes.

Drink the water with the crushed garlic still in it; no straining is needed. Take it with food if raw garlic irritates your stomach.

Easy daily combination

Use soaked chia or ground flax at breakfast, then include 1-2 cups of varied cooked vegetables with lunch or dinner. Chia and flax are high in fiber, so increase gradually if they are new to your diet. Do not swallow dry chia seeds by the spoonful. Raw garlic can cause heartburn or stomach upset and may increase bleeding risk; check with a clinician before regular use if you take blood thinners or have a procedure scheduled. Garlic water is not a proven skin treatment.
