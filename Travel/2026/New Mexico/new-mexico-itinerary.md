# New Mexico Road Trip

**Dates:** October 10-12, 2026  
**Route:** Seattle - Albuquerque - Santa Fe - White Sands - El Paso - Seattle  
**Trip style:** Outdoors, scenery, food, culture, and walking - no museums  
**Average high:** 70-72°F (21-22°C)  
**Average low:** 43-48°F (6-9°C)  
**Sunset:** 6:51 PM

## Saturday, October 10 - Day 1: Albuquerque and Santa Fe

**Route:** [Open in Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pK4x4eModS3UYHtF6)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:40 AM | Depart Seattle for Albuquerque. |
| 1:52 PM | Arrive at Albuquerque International Sunport. |
| 2:00 PM | Pick up the rental car and grab a quick lunch or snack - about 45 minutes. |
| 2:45 PM | Drive approximately 1 hour to Santa Fe. |
| 3:45 PM | Santa Fe Plaza - walk the historic plaza, admire adobe architecture, browse local shops, and photograph street performers and art vendors. |
| 4:10 PM | Cathedral Basilica of St. Francis of Assisi - enjoy the exterior and surrounding area, with only a quick interior visit if desired. |
| 4:25 PM | Palace of the Governors and surrounding historic streets - exterior walk only. |
| 4:40 PM | Canyon Road - walk among adobe buildings, outdoor sculptures, galleries, and small shops. |
| 5:15 PM | Drive to Balloon Fiesta Park - allow about 1 hour and monitor festival traffic. |
| 6:00 PM | [Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Albuquerque+International+Balloon+Fiesta+Balloon+Fiesta+Park) - enjoy the evening session, hot air balloons, festival grounds, and dinner from the vendors until 9:00 PM. |
| 9:00 PM | Head to the Airbnb in Albuquerque. |

**Stay:** Airbnb in Albuquerque

## Sunday, October 11 - Day 2: White Sands

**Route:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Albuquerque+New+Mexico&destination=El+Paso+Texas&waypoints=PistachioLand+Alamogordo%7CWhite+Sands+National+Park+Visitor+Center%7CInterdune+Boardwalk)

> White Sands is the main adventure. Bring water, sunscreen, sunglasses, snacks, and shoes suitable for hot sand.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | Breakfast - have a New Mexico breakfast burrito with green chile. |
| 7:30 AM | Leave Albuquerque and drive south toward White Sands. |
| 12:00 PM | [PistachioLand](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=PistachioLand+Alamogordo+New+Mexico) - spend 45 minutes seeing the giant pistachio and browsing local pistachio treats. |
| 1:00 PM | White Sands National Park Visitor Center - rent a sand sled and pick up wax from [White Sands Trading Company](https://whitesandstradingcompany.com/), then use the remaining time for restrooms, water, and park information. |
| 1:30 PM | Dunes Drive - 1 hour 30 minutes for scenic pullouts and photography. |
| 3:00 PM | Sand sledding and dunes - 1 hour 30 minutes. |
| 4:30 PM | Interdune Boardwalk - 45-minute easy walk. |
| 5:15 PM | Dune time and sunset setup - explore at an easy pace, then choose a quiet dune with a clear western view. |
| 6:30 PM | Sunset at White Sands - settle in before the 6:51 PM sunset. |
| 7:05 PM | Drive approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to El Paso. |
| 8:35 PM | Check in, have Mexican or Tex-Mex dinner, and relax. |

**Stay:** Accommodation in El Paso
