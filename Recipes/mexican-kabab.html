Mexican-Style Rajma Kebabs

Crisp vegetable kebabs with kidney beans, sweet corn, bell pepper, and smoky spices, served with a cool jalapeño yogurt dip.

Makes 10 kebabs  |  Prep 25 min  |  Cook 15 min

Ingredients

For the kebabs

  • 1 1/2 cups cooked rajma (kidney beans), rinsed and drained well
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels, thawed if frozen
  • 1/2 cup finely diced bell pepper
  • 1/3 cup besan (chickpea flour), plus more if needed
  • 1 tsp green chili paste, or to taste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3/4 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tsp neutral oil, plus more for cooking

For the jalapeño yogurt dip

  • 3/4 cup thick plain yogurt or Greek yogurt
  • 1-2 tbsp finely chopped pickled jalapeño
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Pat the rajma and corn dry. Mash the rajma with a fork or potato masher, leaving a few small pieces for texture. Roughly chop the corn.
  2. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the bell pepper and corn for 3-4 minutes, until their excess moisture evaporates. Transfer to a mixing bowl and cool slightly.
  3. In the same dry skillet, toast the besan over medium-low heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant. Do not let it brown deeply. Cool for a few minutes.
  4. Add the mashed rajma, toasted besan, green chili paste, cumin, paprika, oregano, salt, coriander, and lime juice to the vegetables. Mix thoroughly, then rest for 10 minutes.
  5. Form 10 compact, 1/2-inch-thick kebabs. If the mixture sticks heavily or will not hold, mix in extra besan 1 tablespoon at a time. Chill the shaped kebabs for 15 minutes if they feel delicate.
  6. For pan-frying, lightly oil a skillet and cook over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side, until crisp and deep golden.
  7. For air-frying, brush both sides with oil and cook at 380°F for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  8. For the dip, stir together the yogurt, jalapeño, paprika, garlic, lime juice, and salt. Chill until ready to serve with the hot kebabs.

Best texture

Keep the ingredients dry and avoid puréeing the rajma. Shape one test kebab first; it should feel compact and moist without sticking heavily to your hands.

Make ahead

Refrigerate shaped, uncooked kebabs for up to 24 hours or cooked ones for up to 3 days. Keep the dip separately refrigerated and use it within 3 days. Contains dairy unless plant-based yogurt is used.
