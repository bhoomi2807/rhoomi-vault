Freezer recipe

Refreshing Herb Cubes

Bright coriander and mint cubes with ginger, chili, cumin, and chaat masala. Drop one into water, chaas, or lemonade for instant flavor.

Makes about 12 standard ice cubes

Ingredients

  • 1 packed cup fresh coriander leaves and tender stems
  • 1/2 packed cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
  • 1 small green chili, chopped
  • 1 tsp roasted ground cumin (jeera)
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/4 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 3/4 cup cold water, plus more if needed

Method

  1. Wash the coriander and mint thoroughly, then drain well. Pick out any tough coriander stems.
  2. Add the herbs, ginger, green chili, cumin, chaat masala, salt, and water to a blender. Blend until very smooth, adding 1-2 tbsp more water only if needed.
  3. Taste and adjust the salt or chaat masala. Remember that chaat masala is already salty.
  4. Pour into a clean ice-cube tray and freeze until solid, about 6 hours or overnight.
  5. Transfer the frozen cubes to a labeled freezer bag or airtight container and use within 2 months.

How to serve

Add 1 cube to 8-12 oz cold water, sparkling water, chaas, or lemonade. Let it soften for a few minutes, stir, and adjust with more water if the flavor is too concentrated.

For a tangier cube, blend in 2 tbsp lemon or lime juice. Reduce the green chili to half for a milder batch.
