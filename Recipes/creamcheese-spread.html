Homemade savory spread

Jalapeño Paneer Spread

Fresh, softly drained paneer blended with butter and turmeric, then folded with jalapeños and chili flakes.

Makes about 1 1/2 cups  |  Prep 20 min  |  Chill 1 hr

Ingredients

For the soft paneer

  • 1.5 L whole milk
  • 1 1/2 tsp lemon juice, plus more as needed
  • 2 tbsp water, for diluting the lemon juice

For the spread

  • 2 tsp softened butter
  • 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 2-4 tbsp reserved whey, as needed
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped pickled jalapeños, well drained
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes, or to taste

Method

  1. Line a sieve with cheesecloth and set it over a large bowl. Dilute the 1 1/2 teaspoons lemon juice with 2 tablespoons water.
  2. Bring the milk just to a boil in a heavy pot, stirring often to prevent scorching. Turn the heat to low and slowly stir in the diluted lemon.
  3. Stir gently for 30 seconds, then remove from the heat and wait 2 minutes. The curds should separate from pale yellow-green whey. If the whey is still milky, add more lemon juice 1 teaspoon at a time until it clears.
  4. Pour into the lined sieve. Reserve some whey, then rinse the curds briefly under cool water to remove the lemon taste. Gather the cloth and drain for 10-15 minutes without pressing; the paneer should remain soft and moist.
  5. While the paneer is still slightly warm, place it in a blender or food processor with the butter, turmeric, and salt. Blend until smooth, adding reserved whey 1 tablespoon at a time to reach a creamy, spreadable consistency.
  6. Transfer to a bowl and fold in the drained jalapeños and chili flakes. Taste and adjust the salt or heat.
  7. Spoon into a clean airtight container and refrigerate immediately for at least 1 hour before serving.

Skip the Eno

Eno is a leavening fruit salt intended for batters, not a preservative or stabilizer for dairy spreads. It can create an alkaline or fizzy flavor here, so this recipe uses reserved whey for smoothness instead.

Keep refrigerated

Use pasteurized milk and clean utensils. Refrigerate the spread within 2 hours of preparation at 40°F (4°C) or below, use within 3 days, and discard it if left at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
