Soy & Oat Cutlets

Crisp-edged, warmly spiced patties made with minced soy chunks, ground oats, onion, and fresh coriander.

Makes 10 cutlets  |  Prep 20 min  |  Cook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry soy chunks
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats, blended into flour
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2-3 tsp neutral oil, plus more for pan-frying

Method

  1. Cover the soy chunks with boiling water and soak for 10 minutes, or cook according to the package. Drain, rinse under cold water, and squeeze them very dry.
  2. Pulse the squeezed soy chunks in a food processor until finely minced. Do not blend them into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 3-4 minutes until softened. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, until it no longer smells raw. Cool slightly.
  4. Combine the minced soy, onion mixture, oat flour, salt, chili powder, garam masala, chaat masala, coriander, and lemon juice. Mix firmly, then rest for 10 minutes so the oats absorb moisture.
  5. Divide into 10 portions and press into compact 1/2-inch-thick patties. If the mixture feels wet, add oat flour 1 tablespoon at a time; if it crumbles, mix in water 1 teaspoon at a time.
  6. For pan-frying, coat a skillet lightly with oil and cook over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side, until deep golden and hot throughout.
  7. For air-frying, brush both sides lightly with oil and cook at 380°F for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway through.

Keep them together

Squeezing the soy chunks thoroughly is the key step. Shape one test cutlet after the mixture rests, then adjust with oat flour or water before forming the rest.

Serve + store

Serve hot with coriander chutney, yogurt, or ketchup. Refrigerate cooked cutlets for up to 3 days and reheat in a skillet or air fryer. The recipe contains soy and oats.
