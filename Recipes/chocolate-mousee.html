No-bake high-protein dessert

Chocolate Protein Mousse

Silken tofu and dark chocolate blend into a deeply chocolatey, creamy mousse that firms beautifully in the refrigerator.

Makes 4 servings  |  Prep 10 min  |  Chill 2 hr

Ingredients

  • 308 g silken tofu, drained
  • 2 scoops chocolate protein powder
  • 2 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 80 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt
  • Dash of vanilla extract

Method

  1. Let the drained silken tofu sit at room temperature for 15-20 minutes. Pat away any excess liquid.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate in a dry microwave-safe bowl in 20-second bursts, stirring after each, until smooth. Let it cool for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Add the tofu, protein powder, cocoa powder, honey, salt, and vanilla to a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
  4. With the blender running on low, stream in the melted chocolate. Blend for another 30-45 seconds until glossy and completely smooth.
  5. Divide among four small serving cups. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or until chilled and set.

For a silky texture

Room-temperature tofu keeps the melted chocolate fluid while blending. If the mousse is too thick, blend in milk 1 teaspoon at a time. Protein powders vary, so add only as much as needed.

Storage

Keep covered in the refrigerator and enjoy within 3 days. Serve cold, plain or topped with berries or a little shaved dark chocolate. Contains soy; other allergens depend on the protein powder and chocolate.
