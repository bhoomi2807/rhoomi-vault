Balanced vegetarian meal

Hearty Rajma Paneer Bowl

Warm rice and kidney beans layered with golden paneer, corn, onion, and bell pepper, finished with a creamy almond-chili yogurt dressing.

Serves 4  |  Prep 20 min  |  Cook 15 min

Ingredients

For the bowls

  • 2 cups cooked kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 250 g paneer, cut into bite-size cubes
  • 1 cup cooked corn kernels
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 large bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

For the almond-chili dressing

  • 1/3 cup raw almonds
  • 1 dried red chili, stemmed
  • 3/4 cup plain yogurt or Greek yogurt
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 2-4 tbsp water, as needed

Method

  1. Place the almonds and dried red chili in a heatproof bowl. Cover with boiling water and soak for 20 minutes, then drain. For milder heat, shake the seeds out of the softened chili.
  2. Blend the soaked almonds and chili with the yogurt, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and 2 tablespoons water until completely smooth. Add more water gradually for a drizzleable consistency. Refrigerate.
  3. Warm the rice and kidney beans separately until steaming hot. Season the beans with a pinch each of cumin, paprika, and salt.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the paneer in one layer and cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden. Transfer to a plate.
  5. In the same skillet, cook the onion and bell pepper for 4-5 minutes, until lightly charred but still crisp. Add the corn, remaining cumin, paprika, and salt; cook for 2 minutes.
  6. Return the paneer to the skillet, add 1 tablespoon lemon juice, and toss gently for 30 seconds. Remove from the heat.
  7. Divide the hot rice among four bowls. Arrange the kidney beans and paneer-vegetable mixture on top, then drizzle with the cold almond-chili yogurt dressing just before serving.

Build good contrast

Dry the paneer before searing and avoid crowding the skillet. Keep the vegetables slightly crisp and add the chilled dressing only at the end so the bowl has warm, cool, creamy, and crisp elements.

Meal prep safely

Cool cooked rice promptly and refrigerate it within 1 hour. Store the bowl components and dressing separately for up to 3 days. Reheat rice, beans, paneer, and vegetables until steaming hot; serve dressing cold.
