Chocolate Date-Nut Ice Cream

A rich chocolate frozen dessert made creamy with almonds, cashews, oats, and dates. No additional sugar is needed.

Makes about 3 cups  |  2 standard CREAMi pints

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup raw almonds
  • 1/4 cup raw cashews
  • 1/4 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 7-8 soft pitted dates, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 cup dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 cups hot milk of choice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional
  • 1/8 tsp salt

Method

  1. Place the almonds, cashews, oats, and chopped dates in a heatproof bowl. Pour over the hot milk, cover, and soak for 30 minutes.
  2. While the mixture is still warm, add the chopped dark chocolate and stir until melted. Let it cool until only slightly warm.
  3. Transfer everything to a high-speed blender. Add the cocoa powder, vanilla, and salt, then blend for 60-90 seconds until completely smooth. Scrape the sides and blend again if needed.
  4. Cool the mixture fully in the refrigerator. Divide it between two standard Ninja CREAMi pint containers, staying below each MAX FILL line and smoothing the tops flat.
  5. Cover and freeze upright on a level surface for at least 24 hours.
  6. Process one frozen pint at a time on Ice Cream. If crumbly, make a narrow hole in the center, add 1 tbsp milk, and select Re-Spin.

Before freezing

Do not put the hot mixture directly into the freezer. Cooling it in the refrigerator first protects freezer temperature and helps the base freeze evenly. Never process a pint with a sloped or uneven frozen top.

Without a CREAMi

Freeze in a shallow covered container, stirring vigorously every 45 minutes for the first 3 hours. Freeze until firm, then soften at room temperature for 10-15 minutes before scooping.
