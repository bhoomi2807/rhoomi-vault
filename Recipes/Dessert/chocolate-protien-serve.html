Ninja CREAMi recipe

Chocolate Protein Serve

Deep chocolate flavor with a creamy soft-serve texture, built from protein chocolate milk and whey protein.

Makes 1 standard pint  |  Freeze for 24 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups Silk Protein Chocolate milk
  • 1 scoop whey protein powder (about 30 g), chocolate or vanilla
  • 1 tbsp dark cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp allulose, or to taste
  • 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
  • Pinch of salt, optional

Method

  1. Add the chocolate milk, whey protein, cocoa, allulose, xanthan gum, and salt to a blender. Blend for 20-30 seconds until completely smooth and no cocoa pockets remain.
  2. Taste the mixture and add a little more allulose if needed. Frozen desserts taste less sweet once frozen.
  3. Pour into a standard Ninja CREAMi pint, keeping the mixture below the MAX FILL line. Smooth the top flat.
  4. Cover and freeze upright on a level surface for at least 24 hours.
  5. Install the frozen pint in the outer bowl and select Lite Ice Cream.
  6. If powdery, make a narrow hole in the center, add 1 tbsp chocolate milk, and select Re-Spin. For softer soft serve, add a second tablespoon and Re-Spin once more.

Texture note

Measure the xanthan gum carefully. 1/8 teaspoon is enough for one pint; more can make the mixture stretchy or gummy.

Protein powder

Protein powders absorb liquid differently. If your whey scoop is larger than 30 g or contains thickeners, start with 3/4 scoop and adjust the next batch if needed.
