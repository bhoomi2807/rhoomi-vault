Gujarati-inspired recipe

Protein Muthiya

Savory steamed dumplings made with tofu, Greek yogurt, besan, oats, and vegetables, finished with a crisp mustard-sesame tempering.

Serves 4  |  Makes about 24 pieces

Ingredients

For the muthiya

  • 8 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and finely crumbled
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup besan (chickpea flour)
  • 1/2 cup oat flour
  • 1 1/2 cups grated bottle gourd or zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped spinach or methi leaves
  • 1 tbsp ginger-green chili paste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar, optional
  • 1 tsp fruit salt (Eno), added last

For the tempering

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 8-10 curry leaves
  • 1 green chili, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander

Method

  1. Bring 2 inches of water to a simmer in a steamer. Lightly grease the steamer plate and your hands.
  2. Mash the tofu until no large pieces remain. Mix it with the Greek yogurt, squeezed vegetable, spinach, ginger-chili paste, lemon juice, spices, salt, and sugar.
  3. Add the besan and oat flour. Mix into a soft, slightly sticky dough that holds its shape. If wet, add besan 1 tbsp at a time; if dry, add Greek yogurt 1 tbsp at a time.
  4. Sprinkle in the fruit salt and mix gently just until combined. Immediately divide and shape into 4 logs about 1 1/2 inches thick.
  5. Place the logs apart on the steamer plate. Cover and steam over medium heat for 20-24 minutes, until firm and a toothpick comes out without wet batter.
  6. Cool for 10 minutes, then cut each log into 1/2-inch slices. Cooling before slicing prevents crumbling.
  7. Heat the oil in a wide pan. Add mustard seeds; once they pop, add sesame seeds, curry leaves, and sliced chili. Cook for 20 seconds.
  8. Add the sliced muthiya and toss gently for 3-4 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden. Finish with chopped coriander and serve.

Texture matters

Press the tofu and squeeze the grated vegetable very well. Their water content varies, so use the dough's ability to hold a log as the final guide rather than adding all the extra flour at once.

Serve + store

Serve warm with cilantro chutney and extra Greek yogurt. Refrigerate for up to 4 days; reheat in a covered pan or air-fry at 375°F until warm and lightly crisp.
