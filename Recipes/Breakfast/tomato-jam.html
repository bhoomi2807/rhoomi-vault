Spicy Tomato Jam

A sweet, tangy, gently spicy jam for toast, burgers, grilled cheese, roasted vegetables, and snack boards. Makes about 2 cups.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ripe tomatoes, cored and diced
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp Calabrian chili paste
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a wide, heavy-bottomed saucepan and stir until the tomatoes are evenly coated.
  2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring often. Reduce the heat to maintain a lively simmer.
  3. Cook uncovered for 35-50 minutes, stirring more frequently as the mixture thickens so the sugar does not scorch.
  4. The jam is ready when it looks glossy and a spoon dragged across the bottom leaves a trail that closes slowly. It will thicken more as it cools.
  5. Taste carefully and adjust with salt or lemon juice. Cool, transfer to clean jars, and refrigerate.

Chili paste substitutes

Closest option: use 2 tsp sambal oelek plus 1 tsp olive oil. You can also use 1 tbsp harissa, or 1-2 tsp red pepper flakes plus 2 tsp olive oil. Start with less and adjust to taste.

Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months. This recipe has not been tested for shelf-stable canning.
