Ninja CREAMi recipe

Thick Acai Bowl

A spoonable, fruit-forward acai base with enough body for generous toppings. Blend today, spin tomorrow, and eat it straight away.

Makes 1 standard pint  |  Freeze for 24 hours

Ingredients

Acai base

  • 2 unsweetened acai puree packets (about 7 oz total), thawed
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1/2 cup apple juice or unsweetened almond or oat milk
  • Up to 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey, optional
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt

Topping ideas

  • Sliced banana or fresh berries
  • Granola
  • Unsweetened coconut flakes
  • Chia seeds or ground flax seeds
  • Peanut or almond butter

Method

  1. Blend the thawed acai, banana, chosen liquid, lime juice, and salt until completely smooth. Taste and add maple syrup only if needed; apple juice will already add sweetness.
  2. Pour into a standard Ninja CREAMi pint container. Keep the mixture below the MAX FILL line and smooth the top flat.
  3. Cover and freeze the pint upright on a level surface for at least 24 hours.
  4. Remove the lid, place the pint in the outer bowl, install the paddle and lid, and lock the bowl into the machine. Select Smoothie Bowl.
  5. If the mixture looks powdery after the first spin, make a small hole in the center, add 1 tbsp of your chosen liquid, and select Re-Spin. Repeat once only if needed.
  6. Spoon into a chilled bowl, add the toppings, and serve immediately.

For the best texture

Apple juice makes a brighter, fruitier bowl; plant milk makes it creamier and less sweet. Use unsweetened frozen acai puree rather than acai juice, and blend all base ingredients before the 24-hour freeze.

Machine note

If your CREAMi model does not have a Smoothie Bowl setting, use Lite Ice Cream, then Re-Spin with 1 tbsp of the chosen liquid if needed. Follow the capacity marks for your specific pint.
