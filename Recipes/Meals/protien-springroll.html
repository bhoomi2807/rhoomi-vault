Air-fryer recipe

Protein Spring Rolls

Crisp rice-paper rolls filled with browned tofu, colorful vegetables, and edamame, served with a bright soy, lemon, and chili dip.

Makes 8 rolls  |  Serves 4

Ingredients

Filling + wrappers

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup shelled edamame, thawed and roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp neutral oil, plus oil spray for air-frying
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 1/2 cups finely shredded cabbage
  • 1 medium carrot, cut into thin matchsticks
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 8 large round rice-paper wrappers

Soy-lemon dip

  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1-2 tsp chili oil, to taste
  • 1 green onion, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1-2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Heat 1 tsp oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until its moisture cooks off and the edges brown.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger; stir for 30 seconds. Add the cabbage, carrot, bell pepper, and edamame. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender and dry, not watery.
  3. Stir in the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and green onions. Cook until no liquid remains, then spread the filling on a plate and cool completely.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 380°F. Fill a wide shallow bowl with cool water and lightly oil a plate or cutting board.
  5. Dip one rice-paper sheet in water for only 3-5 seconds; it should still feel firm. Set it on the oiled surface and wait about 20 seconds for it to soften.
  6. Place about 1/3 cup filling below the center. Fold the bottom over the filling, fold in both sides, and roll snugly without pulling hard. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling.
  7. Spray all sides lightly with oil. Arrange seam-side down in the basket with space between each roll; cook in batches if needed.
  8. Air-fry for 10-12 minutes, carefully turning after 6 minutes, until blistered and crisp. Let rest for 2 minutes before lifting from the basket.
  9. Whisk all dip ingredients together, starting with 1 tbsp water. Taste and add the second tablespoon if you prefer a lighter dip. Serve at once.

For crisp rice paper

Keep the filling dry and fully cooled, soften wrappers briefly, and prevent the rolls from touching in the basket. Rice-paper brands vary, so check early and add 1-2 minutes only if needed.

Easy substitutions

Replace edamame with more tofu or cooked crumbled tempeh. Use tamari for gluten-free rolls and maple syrup instead of honey for a fully vegan dip.
