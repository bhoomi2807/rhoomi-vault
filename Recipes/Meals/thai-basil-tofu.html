Thai Basil Tofu

Crisp-edged crumbled tofu, fiery garlic, and plenty of fragrant basil in a savory-sweet sauce. Fast, bold, and built for jasmine rice.

Serves 3  |  Ready in 30 minutes

Ingredients

Stir-fry

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, drained and patted dry
  • 2 tbsp neutral high-heat oil, divided
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2-4 Thai chilies, to taste
  • 1 mild red chili or 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 packed cup holy basil or Thai basil leaves
  • Cooked jasmine rice, for serving

Sauce

  • 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetarian oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 tsp brown or palm sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1/4 tsp ground white pepper

Method

  1. Whisk all sauce ingredients in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves. Have every ingredient ready before heating the pan.
  2. Pound the garlic and Thai chilies into a rough paste with a mortar and pestle. Alternatively, mince them very finely together.
  3. Crumble the tofu into bite-size, irregular pieces. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Add the tofu in one layer and leave it undisturbed for 2-3 minutes to brown.
  4. Stir-fry for another 4-5 minutes until the moisture has cooked off and several edges are golden. Transfer the tofu to a plate.
  5. Lower the heat to medium-high and add the remaining oil. Fry the garlic-chili paste for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Do not let the garlic burn.
  6. Return the tofu to the pan. Add the sauce and sliced mild chili, then toss over high heat for 1-2 minutes until glossy and nearly dry.
  7. Turn off the heat. Add the basil and toss only until wilted. Taste and adjust with soy sauce, sugar, or a splash of water. Serve at once over jasmine rice.

Smart substitutions

Holy basil gives the most traditional peppery aroma. Thai basil is floral and anise-like; regular Italian basil is also a good substitute. Use serrano chilies if Thai chilies are unavailable. For gluten-free, use tamari and a certified gluten-free vegetarian oyster sauce.

Research note

The high-heat browning, garlic-chili paste, restrained sauce, and basil-off-heat method were adapted for tofu after reviewing Hot Thai Kitchen's pad kra pao guide. This vegetarian version is an adaptation, not a traditional meat recipe.
