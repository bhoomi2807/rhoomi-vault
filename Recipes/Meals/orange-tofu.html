Air-Fried Orange Tofu

Crisp, seasoned tofu tossed in a glossy orange, ginger, and garlic sauce.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, cubed
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, plus more for the air fryer
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/4 tsp chili flakes, or to taste

Method

  1. Bring salted water to a gentle boil. Add the tofu and simmer for 3-5 minutes. Drain, cool briefly, and pat very dry.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 430°F. Toss the tofu with cornstarch, onion powder, garlic powder, and a light coating of oil.
  3. Air-fry for 18 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until crisp and golden.
  4. With the stove still off, add the neutral oil, green onions, soy sauce, brown sugar, orange juice, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and chili flakes to a large pan. Stir until combined.
  5. Turn the heat to medium. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring, until slightly thickened.
  6. Add the crispy tofu and toss until evenly coated. Cook for another minute, then serve immediately.
